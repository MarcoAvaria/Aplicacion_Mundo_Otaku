## A. Yoru Exchange

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: wide brand key visual for a responsive web and mobile app design proposal
Primary request: create an original sophisticated manga-inspired night city illustration for “Mundo Otaku”, a community where fans exchange manga, figures, clothing, and collectibles. Show an atmospheric urban passage with subtle floating exchange motifs such as two abstract cards or parcels moving between people, but no readable labels and no recognizable copyrighted characters.
Style/medium: premium editorial anime-inspired illustration mixed with subtle screen-tone and risograph grain; mature portfolio presentation, clean enough to sit behind UI
Composition/framing: 16:9 wide landscape, main visual energy on the right half, generous calm negative space on the left for interface copy, layered depth, no device mockup
Lighting/mood: welcoming dusk, luminous storefront glow, community feeling, polished rather than childish
Color palette: near-black indigo, muted violet, warm coral, soft cream, tiny mint highlights
Constraints: original characters only; no existing anime franchises; no logos; no legible text; no watermark; avoid sexualized characters, weapons, clutter, and oversaturated neon
```

## B. Manga Press

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: wide brand key visual for a second responsive app design proposal
Primary request: create an original graphic editorial illustration for “Mundo Otaku”, a community marketplace for exchanging manga and collectibles. Depict two anonymous hands exchanging a manga-sized book and a small collectible box over a bold abstract city map.
Style/medium: contemporary Japanese print design, risograph layers, manga screen-tone, crisp paper collage, flat geometric forms, premium cultural magazine aesthetic
Composition/framing: 16:9 landscape; central exchange gesture; generous uncluttered cream area on the left for UI copy; strong graphic rhythm; no device mockup
Lighting/mood: energetic, confident, collectible, tactile
Color palette: warm paper cream, carbon black, vermilion red, muted plum, one small cobalt accent
Materials/textures: visible ink registration, halftone dots, recycled paper grain
Constraints: no logos, no readable text, no recognizable copyrighted characters or franchises, no watermark, no weapons, no sexualized figures, avoid photorealism and neon cyberpunk
```

## C. Club Coleccionista

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: wide brand key visual for a third responsive app design proposal
Primary request: create an original bright, cozy illustration for “Mundo Otaku”, a friendly platform for exchanging manga, figures, apparel, and collectibles. Show a sunlit collector’s room with carefully organized shelves and two diverse young adult friends exchanging a book and a small parcel.
Style/medium: polished soft 3D illustration blended with clean editorial shapes, modern product-design aesthetic, mature and welcoming rather than childlike
Composition/framing: 16:9 landscape; people and collection on the right two-thirds; calm pale negative space on the left for UI copy; clear depth and uncluttered surfaces; no device mockup
Lighting/mood: warm morning daylight, trustworthy, calm, community-oriented
Color palette: soft cream, desaturated teal, clay coral, dusty lavender, natural wood, charcoal accents
Materials/textures: matte paper, warm wood, soft fabric, subtle ceramic
Constraints: original characters and objects only; no recognizable copyrighted franchises; no logos; no readable text; no watermark; no weapons; no sexualized characters; avoid neon cyberpunk and visual clutter
```
